Nexora AI Notes for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Nexora AI Notes records and transcribes meetings, creates summaries and action items, and answers questions about transcripts. You do not need a Nexora account. The app processes meeting content on your device and does not send it to a Nexora server for transcription or AI generation.

What the app stores and why

  • Recordings and imported media. The app uses the microphone when you start a recording. It reads the audio or video you choose to import or send to it through the system share sheet. The app may copy selected media into its local storage and prepare an audio copy for transcription and playback.
  • Meeting library. The app stores meeting audio, transcripts, summaries, action items, questions and answers, and details such as titles, dates, durations, and categories. It uses this data to display, edit, search, play, and export your meetings.
  • Preferences and trial usage. The app saves settings such as language, appearance, and model choices locally. It tracks the five free recording saves using local storage, including the iOS Keychain. Deleting a meeting does not reset trial usage.

On-device processing and model downloads

Qwen speech recognition and the Qwen AI engine run locally. If you choose Apple speech transcription or Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple’s system speech or on-device language model. For example, the selected AI engine uses relevant transcript text to generate a summary or answer a question.

iOS may download Apple speech assets. If you download the optional Qwen speech model through the app, it fetches model files from Hugging Face or its delivery infrastructure. The download provider can receive standard connection information such as your IP address. These model requests do not include your meeting audio, transcripts, or notes.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes Nexora Plus subscriptions through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to check transaction and entitlement information and restore eligible purchases. It does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

When you export a meeting and select a destination in the iOS share sheet, the exported file is passed to the app, service, or recipient you choose. Their handling of that copy is governed by their own practices. The app does not offer account-based cloud sync. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by iOS and may contain app data.

Permissions, retention, and deletion

You can change Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions for Nexora AI Notes in iOS Settings. Turning off a permission prevents the corresponding future access; it does not delete existing data.

Saved meetings stay in the local library until you remove them. Use the meeting’s menu to delete it, or use Settings → Clear all data to clear the meeting library. This is not a device-wide erase. Original files outside the app, exported copies, backups, and some separately stored source files, including legacy Apple Watch audio, may remain. Remove those copies separately where they are stored. If a downloaded Qwen model is installed, its management screen provides a separate removal option.

Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any files you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot, and retain the correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending private recordings, transcripts, or payment details.

These pages do not add tracking scripts or set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process standard request information as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Privacy questions or requests

Email allanhyh@qq.com for privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to support. We cannot access or remotely delete meeting data that remains only on your device.
