Recording and transcription

Recording won’t start

Check that Nexora AI Notes has Microphone permission in iOS Settings. If you use Apple speech transcription, check Speech Recognition permission as well. If you have used all five free recording saves, open Settings → Nexora Plus to check subscription options.

A transcript is missing or failed

Open the meeting’s Transcript tab and use Retry Transcription if shown. Choose the right transcription language. For imported files, you can select Apple or Qwen as the file transcription engine. If Qwen is unavailable, check its status in Settings → Speech transcription → Qwen model. Apple speech assets and optional model downloads may require an internet connection.

Summaries, questions, and exports

Open a meeting with a transcript and choose Summary to generate an AI summary or Q&A to ask a question. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, check its availability on your device or select the built-in Qwen engine in Settings → On-device AI. New AI generation requires remaining trial access or an active subscription.

Use the meeting’s Export action to create a PDF, Markdown, or plain text file. Choose which sections to include, then use the iOS share sheet to save or send it.

Nexora Plus

Unsubscribed users can save up to five trial recordings. Closing the subscription offer lets you return to your library. After trial access is used up or a subscription ends, saved meetings remain available to view, play, edit, and export.

To restore a subscription, open Settings → Nexora Plus → Restore Purchases and use the Apple Account that originally purchased it. For billing, renewal, or cancellation, use Apple’s subscription settings. The app displays your storefront price before you purchase.

Managing your local library

Delete a single meeting from its menu, or open Settings → Clear all data to clear the library. Export important material before deleting it. Deleting meetings does not remove copies you previously exported or files you originally imported from another location.

For details on data processing and retention, read the Privacy Policy.
