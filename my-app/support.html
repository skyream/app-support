PhotoGlide Support

Use this guide to troubleshoot PhotoGlide and get back to organizing your photo library.

Photo access

Check PhotoGlide’s permissions in iOS Settings. If you granted limited access, only selected photos and videos appear.

Sorting and deletion

Swipe right to keep an item or left to mark it for deletion. In the review screen, undo individual marks or select several items and remove them from the deletion list. Items are deleted through Apple Photos only after confirmation.

AI and similar photos

Run AI classification to build the on-device index. Similar Photo Groups searches indexed photos and suggests a photo to keep; it does not mark or delete items.

Cache

Clear Cache in More removes in-memory thumbnails while keeping sorting records and classification results.
