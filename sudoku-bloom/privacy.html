Sudoku Bloom

Privacy Policy

Gameplay data

Sudoku Bloom stores puzzle progress, notes, unlocked difficulty levels, hint usage, and preferences in local app storage so you can resume play. The app does not require an account or send this gameplay data to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device.

Subscriptions

Apple processes purchases and subscription payments through the App Store. Sudoku Bloom uses Apple StoreKit to check purchase entitlements and restore access. The app does not receive your payment card details. For details about Apple's handling of App Store data, see App Store & Privacy.

Analytics and advertising

Sudoku Bloom does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your email address and any information you choose to provide to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords or payment information. For privacy questions, contact us through the support page.
