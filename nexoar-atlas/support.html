Nexoar Atlas Support

Help with object capture, spatial scanning, on-device reconstruction, saved projects, and subscriptions.

Contact support

Write to gameskydream@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, scanning mode, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain private information.

Email support

Never include your Apple Account password or payment-card details. Share scan files only if you are comfortable with their contents.

Choose a scanning mode

  • Object Scan: Apple Object Capture for supported devices. Move around the object and follow the capture guidance.
  • LiDAR and Detailed Scan: capture spatial geometry using LiDAR; a compatible LiDAR-equipped device is required.
  • Room Scan: uses Apple RoomPlan to capture room structure on supported devices.
  • Camera Scan and Photo Reconstruction: rear-camera capture and photo-based reconstruction. Availability depends on the device and processing capabilities.
  • Front Depth Scan: requires an available TrueDepth camera.

Some modes require newer hardware or iOS features. A subscription unlocks app access but cannot add hardware capabilities to a device.

Improve scan quality

Use even lighting, move slowly, and capture overlapping views. Keep the subject still and in frame. Transparent, shiny, very dark, or textureless surfaces can be difficult to reconstruct. For objects, capture all sides and avoid abrupt changes in distance. Leave enough free storage for source photos and generated models.

Camera unavailable or reconstruction fails

Check camera access in iOS Settings for Nexoar Atlas. Close other apps using the camera, ensure your device supports the selected mode, and retry with a smaller capture. Reconstruction can take time and use significant memory and battery. Keep the app open while it processes. If the issue persists, send us the error message and the scanning mode used.

Free scans and paid access

You can successfully save up to three new scans for free. Cancelled or failed captures do not consume a free scan. Exporting requires an active subscription, including during the free allowance. Once all three free scans have been saved, an active subscription is required to create more scans.

Deleting saved projects does not restore free scans. An active subscription unlocks unlimited scans and exports across supported modes. The subscription page appears at app launch for users without an active subscription; you can close it and use any remaining free scans.

Subscribe, restore, or cancel

The app offers an annual auto-renewing plan when available from the App Store. Refer to Apple's purchase confirmation for the current price, currency, renewal terms, and availability.

  • Restore access: use Restore Purchases on the subscription page while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection may be required.
  • Manage or cancel: use Manage Subscription in the app, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You can also open Apple subscription management.
  • Purchase unavailable: if the annual plan is not available, try again later. A pending purchase does not unlock access until Apple confirms it.
  • Billing or refund: purchases are handled by Apple. Visit Apple's purchase support for an eligible refund request.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. We cannot cancel or refund an Apple purchase directly.

Saved scans, exports, and deletion

Open your saved scan library to view and manage projects. With an active subscription, use the available export or share action and choose your destination through iOS. Export formats depend on the project and mode.

Scans and models are stored on your device. Atlas does not provide an online account or cloud recovery service. Export important projects before removing the app or changing devices, and check your device backup settings. Deleting a project does not delete copies already exported or shared.
