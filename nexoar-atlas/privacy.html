Privacy Policy

Nexoar Atlas · Effective November 13, 2026

Nexoar Atlas captures objects and spaces and reconstructs 3D models on your device. You do not need to create an Atlas account, and the app does not automatically upload your scans to us.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Nexoar Atlas app and its developer ("we," "us") handle information, including when you contact support or visit these pages. Contact us at gameskydream@gmail.com.

2. Scan content and device processing

Depending on the mode you use, the app processes camera images, captured photo sequences or video, depth maps, LiDAR or TrueDepth measurements, camera position and motion, room geometry, point clouds, meshes, textures, and reconstructed 3D models. Projects may also include names, notes, measurements, timestamps, and capture metadata.

These inputs are used to align captures, reconstruct, preview, edit, measure, and save your scans. Processing and project storage take place on your device using Apple frameworks and local processing libraries. We do not operate a cloud reconstruction service or automatically receive your scan content.

3. Camera, motion, depth, and location

Camera access is needed for scanning. Motion and camera-pose information help align images and depth frames. You can control available permissions in iOS Settings; denying a permission may prevent the corresponding scanning feature from working.

Front Depth Scan uses the TrueDepth camera to capture depth and imagery for 3D reconstruction. If you scan a person, the resulting images and geometry may include their face. This data is used for your scan, not to identify people, authenticate identity, build advertising profiles, or train our models. It remains in your local project unless you choose to share it.

The current app does not request location permission. Its optional GPS metadata path reads location only when iOS has already authorized access and GPS tagging is enabled. If available, coordinates and altitude can be saved with scan metadata and included in exported project data. Ordinary scans do not require location access.

4. Free usage and subscriptions

The app stores identifiers of successfully saved free-scan projects in local preferences and the device Keychain to enforce the three-scan allowance. This record is not sent to our servers. Deleting a project does not reset the allowance; the Keychain record may remain after uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

Apple handles purchases and billing through the App Store. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions, including product, expiration, and revocation information, to determine subscription access. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple processes purchase and account information under its Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing, exports, and backups

When you choose to export or share, the selected files are passed to the destination you choose through iOS. Those files may contain images, geometry, notes, or capture metadata. The recipient or selected service handles the files under its own terms and privacy practices. Review the contents before sharing, particularly scans of people or private spaces.

Atlas does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Copies you export, share, or back up are separate from the local project.

6. Diagnostics, third parties, and tracking

The current app does not integrate advertising, cross-app tracking, or a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. App diagnostic messages are written to local system logs; they may contain technical errors or project paths. Apple may handle system diagnostics according to your device privacy settings.

Local libraries support the interface and 3D processing without receiving your scans as an online service. Opening an external help link or choosing a sharing service connects you to that provider, whose privacy policy applies.

7. Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain support records. Our support mailbox uses Gmail, so Google processes email under its Privacy Policy. Please do not send sensitive scans, passwords, or payment-card details.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to provide and secure hosting; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved projects remain on your device until you delete them. Use the scan library to delete projects. Removing the app removes its local app-container data, but does not necessarily remove the Keychain allowance record, Apple purchase records, device backups, or exported copies. Manage those copies with the relevant device setting, recipient, or service. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve your request and follow-up issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us by email. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete scans stored only on your device. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, or a complaint to a data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information sent to support may be processed where our email provider operates. We limit its use to support and related obligations; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children and changes

Atlas is a general-purpose scanning tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. If a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

We may update this policy when app behavior or services change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
