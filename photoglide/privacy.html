PhotoGlide Privacy Policy

Photos and videos

With your permission, PhotoGlide reads photos and videos available to it in Apple Photos, including metadata such as dates, dimensions and embedded locations. You can change access in iOS Settings. With limited access, PhotoGlide can work only with the items you allow.

Photo sorting, AI classification and similarity comparisons run on your device. PhotoGlide does not send your photo library or AI results to a PhotoGlide server. Sorting marks, undo history, settings, statistics and AI indexes are stored locally. A deletion mark does not delete a photo. When you confirm deletion, PhotoGlide asks Apple Photos to perform it.

Apple services and sharing

Apple Photos may download media from iCloud when needed. For location groups, PhotoGlide can send coordinates from photo metadata to Apple’s location lookup service to obtain place names. Apple handles these services under its privacy policy. If you share a statistics report, iOS lets you choose the recipient or destination.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

PhotoGlide does not require a PhotoGlide account or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the current build.

Support and local data

Clearing the app’s thumbnail cache does not clear your sorting history. Removing the app removes its local app data, but does not delete your Apple Photos library. See PhotoGlide Support for troubleshooting.
